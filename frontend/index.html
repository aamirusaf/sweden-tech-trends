Skill Matrix · Free CV check · Updated every Monday

Sweden is hiring in tech. Are your skills on the list?

Skill Matrix is a free tool that reads every tech job ad on Arbetsförmedlingen, Sweden's public employment service. That's thousands of live ads every week, plus every ad since 2008. Drop in your CV and in seconds you'll see how many of those jobs you already fit, which job titles suit you, and the one skill that would open the most new doors.
